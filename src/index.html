FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not here, call +971 52 865 1180 and speak to an engineer rather than a call centre.

Which crane type suits a warehouse or workshop bay?

For light to medium loads in a workshop or warehouse, a single girder EOT crane is usually the most economical choice. For heavy-duty cycles or longer spans, a double girder EOT crane gives more hook height and capacity. Where the building cannot carry a runway, a gantry or semi-gantry crane runs on floor rails instead. For repetitive lifts at one workstation, a wall- or pillar-mounted jib crane is often enough on its own. Send us your span, capacity and duty cycle and we will specify the configuration.

What does an Annual Maintenance Contract cover?

An Aerolift AMC covers scheduled inspections, preventive maintenance and priority support across the contract year. Wear items are checked on a planned cycle, so faults are found during planned downtime rather than mid-shift — and AMC customers move to the front of the queue when a call-out is needed.

How quickly do you respond to a crane breakdown?

Our emergency breakdown service runs 24/7. A rapid-response team attends site for on-site diagnostics and repair, day or night. For a committed response time at your location, call +971 52 865 1180 and we will confirm it against your site and equipment.

Do you install cranes to international standards?

Yes. New overhead crane installation, structural upgrades and modernisation work are carried out to international standards, so the installed equipment stands up to inspection and audit.

Do you rent lifting equipment as well as sell it?

Yes. Alongside supply and installation, Aerolift rents and services lifting equipment — including boom loaders, forklifts and scissor lifts — for projects that do not justify a purchase. Tell us the duration and the load and we will quote the rental.

What lifting capacities do your hoists cover?

The MT-series wire rope hoist range covers 1 to 50 tonnes depending on configuration: foot-mounted 1–5 t, monorail low-headroom and normal-headroom 1–15 t, and double-rail trolley 5–50 t. A foot-mounted true vertical lift version is available at 1.5 t with 4/2 reeving. Models run MT305, MT308, MT312, MT316 and MT525.
