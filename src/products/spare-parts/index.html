Spare Parts

Crane Spare Parts, Stocked for Any Make

When a crane stops, the part decides how long it stays stopped. These are the components we keep close — for our own installations and for cranes supplied by others.

What we stock

Six Categories That Cover Most Downtime

  • Busbar Systems

    The enclosed conductor line that feeds power along the runway. When it wears, faults arrive as mystery trips — intermittent, and blamed on everything else first.

  • Festoon Systems

    The travelling cable loops that power the hoist and trolley. Sun and dust age them faster here than anywhere — worth inspecting before they fail.

  • Electro-Magnetic Brakes

    The brake that holds the load the instant power is cut. Non-negotiable for safety — and a scheduled-replacement item, not a run-to-failure one.

  • Hydraulic Brakes

    Smooth, controlled braking on travel motions — the difference between a load that stops and a load that swings.

  • Complete Brake Sets

    When the crane is down, one boxed set beats sourcing shoes, springs and solenoids separately — one part number, one visit.

  • Limit Switches

    Hoist and travel limits — the small part standing between a normal stop and an incident report. Tested every AMC visit, replaced the day they hesitate.
