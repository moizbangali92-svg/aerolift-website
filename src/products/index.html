Overhead Cranes, Hoists and Spare Parts — Specified for Your Bay

Five ranges cover overhead cranes, hoists and the parts that keep them running in the UAE. Every one is specified against your span, capacity and duty cycle before it is quoted — sized properly the first time.

EOT Cranes

The Workhorse of the Covered Bay

Electric overhead travelling cranes running on elevated runways — the standard answer for repetitive lifting inside workshops, warehouses and production halls.

  • Single girder — light to medium loads, economical for workshops and warehouses
  • Double girder — heavy-duty cycles, greater hook height and longer spans
View the EOT Crane Range

Gantry Cranes

When the Building Cannot Carry a Runway

Gantries run on floor rails instead of elevated runways — the answer for yards, laydown areas and structures that were never designed to take a crane load.

  • Single and double girder gantries — full portal frames on floor rails
  • Single and double girder semi-gantries — one leg on the floor, one end on an existing runway or wall
View the Gantry Crane Range

Jib Cranes

Repetitive Lifts at a Single Workstation

Where one machine, one bench or one loading point does the same lift all day, a jib covers it without occupying the whole bay’s crane.

  • Wall-mounted — 180°–200° rotation, no floor space taken
  • Pillar-mounted — full 360° rotation, positioned wherever the work is
View the Jib Crane Range
