Choosing the configuration

Headroom Is Usually the Deciding Vote

Capacity narrows the field fast. What settles the choice is almost always the space between the beam and the roof — and what the hook has to do at the top of its travel.

  • 01.

    Tight building?

    Low-headroom monorail units tuck the hoist beside the beam instead of under it — hook height you get back for free.

  • 02.

    Heavy load?

    Above 15 t the double-rail trolley takes over, riding on top of double girder cranes up to 50 t.

  • 03.

    Precision placement?

    True vertical lift with 4/2 reeving keeps the hook rising dead straight — no drift as it climbs.
