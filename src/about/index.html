About Aerolift

The Lifting Equipment Company Behind Your Uptime

Aerolift L.L.C. is a Dubai-based lifting equipment company working across all seven emirates — supplying, installing and maintaining the cranes and hoists that production, storage and construction run on.

Who we are

Your Load, Our Lift: We Carry What Matters Most

Aerolift supplies, installs and maintains industrial lifting equipment — EOT cranes, gantry cranes, jib cranes, MT-series wire rope hoists and the spare parts that keep them running. Based in Dubai, we work wherever in the UAE the load is.

Our engineers carry more than twenty years of industrial lifting experience — in service, not just on paper. That experience shows up where it matters: the right duty class chosen at quotation stage, installations that pass inspection the first time, and breakdown diagnoses made on site rather than over the phone.

Specification, supply, installation and long-term maintenance sit with the same company. When something matters at 3 a.m., there is no gap between the supplier and the service contractor for it to fall into.
